- **0 candidates adopted into code/prompt/mapping this phase.** Two Phase 6 documentation corrections were made (TC-2 reclassified as MEASUREMENT_ISSUE; TC-1's mechanism upgraded from INFERRED to OBSERVED via Candidate F).
- **1 candidate (F) fully investigated with a real, isolated, blinded experiment** — result is scientifically clear (structure, not volume, explains most of A's factuality gap) but does not change the production recommendation, since the "fix" (fully-rendered full document) costs more tokens than either A or B/C.
- **2 candidates (C, D) fully resolved with existing data**, no API calls needed, both KEEP with no change.
- **1 candidate (A) resolved by re-examining Phase 6's own larger corpus**, KEEP with no change.
- **3 items (B, E, TC-6) have concrete, evidence-backed candidate fixes identified and precisely scoped, but explicitly DEFERRED** pending a real Stage-2 isolated test, per the standing instruction not to adopt speculative changes without evidence.
