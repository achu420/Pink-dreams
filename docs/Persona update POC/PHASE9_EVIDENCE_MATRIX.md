## Notes on classification discipline

- Findings #5 and #6 are both labeled "fabrication of a person's identity," but are classified differently on purpose: #5 **contradicts existing, fully-documented canon** (Ritu is named, occupied, and storied), which is unambiguous model behavior; #6 **fills a genuine documentation gap** (no childhood-friend canon exists at all, positive or negative), which cannot be "fixed" without a canon decision that this program is not authorized to make unilaterally.
- Finding #7 is the only item in this table backed by **direct, repeatable code execution** (not inference, not a single anecdotal read) confirming the exact mechanism — hence "very high" confidence despite the fix itself failing on wording completeness.
- Finding #8 is a **clean negative result**, not an inconclusive one: 6 matched, blinded pairs, consistent null-to-negative direction. It rules out one specific mechanism; it does not rule out the entire category of prompt-level interventions (see PHASE9_POST_PHASE8_ANALYSIS.md §3.4).
